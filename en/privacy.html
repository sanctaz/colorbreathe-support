Privacy Policy

Updated October 1, 2026 · Applies to version 2.1 · Contact: 306116317@qq.com

In short: ColorBreathe collects no data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics SDK and no tracking across apps or websites. Your artwork, diary and photos stay on your device and are never sent to us.

Nothing collected

The app does not send your content to the developer, and there is no server-side account or copy of your work.

On your device

Line-art detection, Health mood suggestions and exports are all processed on the device.

Your choice

Each system permission is requested only when you use that feature, and declining never blocks coloring or the diary.

On-device content

ColorBreathe requires no account and includes no ads or analytics SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites. Artwork, strokes, layers, palettes, favorites, moods, diary text, attached photos, drafts, Marble Art progress, Bead Box keepsakes and preferences are processed and stored on your device. The app does not send this content to the developer and does not offer automatic cloud sync.

Camera, photos and files

Camera access is requested only when you choose to photograph line art. The app does not record audio. The system photo picker provides only images you select; it does not grant access to your whole library. Processing, compression, cropping, perspective correction and region detection happen on-device. The file picker reads only files you choose. Temporary import copies are cleaned up after cancellation or failure; accepted project and diary assets remain in the app container.

Only choosing Save to Photos requests add-only permission to save the selected image or replay video. Sharing passes the selected file to your chosen destination, which may use the internet and is governed by its own policy.

Optional health processing

Health mood sync is off by default. On supported devices and only after enabling and authorizing it, the app can read the latest State of Mind for the current day through HealthKit to suggest an approximate mood and color. It does not request other health categories or write health data. Original samples, labels and source details are processed in memory only, never uploaded or stored as raw health records. Matched suggestions may become local drafts; saving a diary requires your action and does not overwrite manual choices.

Turning sync off stops reading and clears in-memory results, while saved drafts and entries remain. Manage permissions in Health or system Settings. No readable result does not mean you have no health records. ColorBreathe does not provide diagnosis or medical advice.

Diary lock, widgets and reminders

The diary lock uses system authentication; the app never receives biometric templates. Widgets read display information from a local App Group. Choose what you want visible on your Home Screen or Lock Screen. Reminders are scheduled locally without a developer notification server and do not contain diary text.

Storage, backup and deletion

Diary, draft and diary attachment directories are excluded from system backup. Other projects or preferences may participate in device backups according to your settings. There is no automatic cloud sync. Deleting the app, losing a device or file corruption can cause data loss.

Settings → Data & Backup lets you explicitly export and import .colorbreathe backups of diaries, artwork or all data; imports have a 256 MB limit. Backups may contain private text, photos and artwork, so choose their destination carefully. Plain text, PNG and video exports do not replace a restorable project backup.

Content remains until you delete it, delete the app or the operating system handles the files. Deleting one entry or project does not delete original Photos items, external shared files or independent attachment copies saved in other diary entries. Manage external copies and Apple backups through the relevant services. The developer has no account or artwork backup on a server.

Purchases through Apple

Apple / StoreKit handles optional subscriptions, trial eligibility, lifetime purchases and invitation redemption. The app reads Apple-signed transactions, purchase and expiry dates, and entitlement information on-device to unlock features and restore purchases; it does not upload this information to a developer server. Apple processes billing and the Apple Account under its own privacy policy. The app does not receive payment-card details, and purchase status is not used for advertising, tracking or health profiling.

Artwork preparation

Built-in drawings include AI-assisted artwork. The app does not offer online AI image generation or send your photos, artwork, diary or Marble Art records to AI services.

Support email and website

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you supply, to respond to and resolve your request. Incoming mail uses Tencent QQ Mail, whose services are subject to Tencent's rules. Please avoid unnecessary private diary content, photos, passwords or payment information. Contact the same address to request handling or deletion of feedback we still retain, except where retention is legally required.

These pages use no advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process IP addresses and request logs needed to serve pages. Website visits and email you voluntarily send are separate from the app's local content processing.

Children, contact and changes

ColorBreathe is for general creative and diary use and is not specifically directed to children. Contact 306116317@qq.com. The publisher is the developer listed on the App Store product page. We update this policy and its applicable version when our data handling changes.
