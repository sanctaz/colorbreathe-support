03 / Eight brushes

Every brush has
its own character.

Every brush is free to use. Tap to fill a region, or layer watercolor and mineral textures stroke by stroke. Layers, palettes, undo and redo are always at hand, and compatible iPads support Apple Pencil.

  • PencilLayer twice for more depth
  • WatercolorSoft blooms that deepen as you layer
  • MarkerEven strokes; layer again for richer color
  • CrayonPress more firmly for richer strokes
  • AcrylicCovers the colors beneath
  • SprayLighter at the edges
  • ShadeDarker colors for shadows, lighter for highlights
  • MineralLayer gently to reveal fine mineral grain

Want a shortcut? Turn on assisted coloring and tap a region to fill it with the reference colors—switch back to free painting any time. Assisted coloring is part of Full Access.

05 / Take a playful break

Marble Art

Turn your own drawing into a board of colored marbles. Hold to fire, drag to aim, bank shots off the walls and hit golden rings to charge your skills. Each round lasts up to three minutes.

Finish a round on your own artwork—clear the board or play until time runs out—to permanently unlock assisted coloring for that drawing, even on the free plan.

Marble Art is available outside mainland China.

What's included for free?

12 starter drawings, a daily featured page, every brush, your private diary and backups, plus standard 2048-pixel PNG exports. Replays and share cards include light app branding.

What does Full Access add?

The complete drawing library, assisted coloring, blank canvases, photo and line-art imports, and 4096-pixel and transparent PNG exports, plus replays and share cards without app branding.

How do purchases and restores work?

Choose a subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase; Apple shows the available plans and local prices. Subscriptions renew automatically and eligible accounts get a 7-day trial. Lifetime never renews and doesn't cancel an existing subscription. Valid purchases can be restored in the app.
