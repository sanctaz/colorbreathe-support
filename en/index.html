What can I use for free?

Start with 12 drawings and a daily featured page. Every brush, the diary, backups and standard 2048-pixel PNG exports are included. Replays and share cards include light app branding.

What does Full Access add?

Unlock the complete library, assisted coloring, blank canvases, photo and line-art imports, plus 4096-pixel and transparent PNG exports. Replays and share cards can be exported without app branding.

How do purchases work?

Choose a subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase where available. Apple shows local plans and prices before purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically; eligible accounts may receive a trial. Lifetime does not renew or cancel an existing subscription. Restore valid purchases in the app.
