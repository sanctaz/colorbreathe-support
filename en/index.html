ColorBreathe Support

Support email: 306116317@qq.com. Updated September 13, 2026, for version 2.1.

Devices and price

Requires iOS or iPadOS 18 or later. Version 2.1 offers free features and optional Full Access through Apple in-app purchases. The app includes 11 coloring books and 132 pages, a diary, line-art import, blank canvases, drawing tools, replays and exports. Compatible iPads support Apple Pencil.

Saving and moving to another device

Strokes save automatically. If saving fails, keep the app and original files, check free storage and retry. Settings → Data & Backup exports or imports diaries, artwork or all data using .colorbreathe files, with a 256 MB import limit. Keep a backup before changing devices or deleting the app. Diary directories do not participate in system backup and the app has no automatic cloud sync. PNG, video and text exports are finished outputs, not complete project backups.

Diaries

Start from Write a diary or Read diary on Today. Mood selection is optional; text alone can be saved. Each entry supports up to 500 characters and 20 attachments, including up to 9 photos. Cancelling lets you keep or discard a draft. Open an entry to edit or delete it. Saved diary images are independent copies of the original artwork or Photos item.

Importing and exporting

In My Works, create a blank canvas or import line art through the camera, photo picker or Files. Region detection depends on closed outlines; adjust cropping, threshold or inversion, or continue with brushes. The camera requires a physical device and permission.

The editor exports PNG images, sharing cards and replay videos, and can save selected outputs to Photos. If camera permission was denied, enable it in system Settings. The photo picker supplies only images you select.

Language and accessibility

Settings → Language offers System, Simplified Chinese and English. The Chinese brand is 今日漫 and the English brand is ColorBreathe. The app supports Dynamic Type, VoiceOver and Reduce Motion. Drawing input depends on device hardware and assistive technology.

Get help

Email 306116317@qq.com with your app version, device, OS version, reproduction steps and error text. Remove unnecessary private content from screenshots. We do not automatically receive diary content or diagnostic files, and cannot recover deleted data without a backup.

Read the linked Privacy Policy. Use of the app is covered by Apple's Standard End User License Agreement.

Free features and Full Access

Free access includes 12 starter drawings, one rotating daily page from a fixed 24-page selection, every brush, continued editing of works already started, diaries, backups and standard 2048-pixel PNG export without app branding. Replays and share cards include light app branding. New drawings outside the free selection, new blank canvases, photo/line-art imports and new copies of restricted works require Full Access. Full Access also includes 4096-pixel and transparent PNGs and the option to remove app branding from replays and share cards. Required artwork-license credits remain.

A drawing becomes a continued work when you actually paint in it; merely viewing or favoriting a drawing does not unlock future access. Trial expiry never deletes your drawings or diary. Existing works stay editable, but do not unlock new copies of otherwise restricted drawings.

Plans, trial and renewal

Mainland China plans are CNY 9.90 per month and CNY 49 per year. Lifetime access is CNY 99 for the first 90 days after the China launch, then CNY 199. US plans are USD 2.90 per month and USD 9.90 per year; new lifetime purchases are not offered in the US. Product availability, actual prices and scheduled offer dates are subject to Apple's purchase confirmation and storefront arrangements. Your Apple Account storefront determines the market, not the app's interface language. Changing storefronts does not revoke a lifetime purchase you already own.

Monthly and annual plans offer a 14-day free trial only when Apple confirms eligibility, once per subscription group and with your confirmation. Installing the app alone does not start a trial or a charge. After the trial, the selected subscription renews automatically at the displayed price unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends.

Lifetime access is a one-time purchase with no automatic renewal and includes future standard features and drawing collections in this app. Buying lifetime access does not cancel an existing subscription. Manage or cancel that subscription through your Apple Account to avoid overlapping charges. Family Sharing is not offered for these purchases.

Invitation: one year of Full Access

Open Settings → Full Access → Redeem Invitation Code and use an Apple offer code issued for this campaign. Redemption requires an internet connection and an Apple Account. Only an Apple-verified transaction unlocks access. Successful redemption grants one free year of Full Access from the Apple transaction date, measured as a calendar anniversary in UTC. Access returns to free features after that year, without automatic renewal or charges. Your artwork and diary remain.

The deadline for redeeming a code is separate from the one-year access period after redemption. Refer to the invitation terms and Apple's eligibility confirmation. Each Apple Account can redeem one code per offer; do not publicly share a one-time-use code. An invitation does not cancel or pause an existing auto-renewing subscription. Cancel that subscription in your Apple Account if you do not want its charges to continue.

Restore, manage and refunds

Open Settings → Full Access → Restore Purchases using the Apple Account used to buy. Manage subscriptions there or in Apple account settings. Purchase, invitation redemption and restore need access to Apple services; verified entitlements are cached on-device for offline use. An expired or refunded purchase returns to free features. If a purchase is awaiting approval, access begins only after approval. Apple manages refund requests at Report a Problem.
