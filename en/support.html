Getting started

Which devices and systems are supported?

iPhone or iPad with iOS / iPadOS 26 or later. Compatible iPads support Apple Pencil in portrait and landscape.

What is free?

Free access includes 12 starter drawings (Birds on Branches), one rotating daily page from a fixed 24-page selection, every brush, continued editing of works you have started, the diary, backups and standard 2048-pixel PNG export without app branding. Replays and share cards include light app branding.

A drawing becomes a work you can keep editing once you actually paint in it; viewing, previewing or favoriting it does not count.

What does Full Access include?
  • All 11 collections and 132 drawings
  • New blank canvases and importing your own photos or line art
  • 4096-pixel and transparent PNG export
  • The option to remove app branding from replays and share cards

Credits required by artwork licenses always remain. When a trial or subscription ends, your artwork and diary stay; existing works stay editable, but you can't start new copies of otherwise restricted drawings.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Coloring, the diary, exports and backups all happen on your device. Only purchases, restoring purchases and redeeming codes need Apple's services; verified access is cached on-device so it keeps working offline.

Purchases, trials and subscriptions

What plans are there and what do they cost?

United States: USD 2.90 per month or USD 9.90 per year; new lifetime purchases are not offered. Mainland China: CNY 9.90 per month or CNY 49 per year, plus lifetime access at CNY 99 for the first 90 days after the China launch, then CNY 199.

Actual prices, availability and offer dates are whatever Apple shows when you confirm a purchase. Your Apple Account storefront decides the market, not the app's language, and changing storefronts does not revoke a lifetime purchase you already own.

How does the free trial work? Will I be charged?

The monthly and annual plans offer a 14-day free trial when Apple confirms you are eligible, once per subscription group, and only after you confirm it on Apple's purchase sheet. Installing the app does not start a trial or a charge.

After the trial, the plan renews automatically. Within five days of a trial ending or a renewal, the Today screen shows the plan, date, price and how to cancel; if notifications are on, you also get one local reminder.

How do I turn off auto-renewal?

Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends: in the app, Settings → Full Access → Manage Apple Subscriptions, or iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. You keep Full Access for the rest of the period you paid for.

How is lifetime access different from a subscription?

Lifetime access (mainland China only) is a one-time purchase with no renewal, and includes future standard features and drawing collections in this app. Buying it does not cancel an existing subscription, so cancel that in your Apple Account to avoid overlapping charges. Family Sharing is not offered for these purchases.

How do I restore a purchase on a new or reset device?

Sign in with the Apple Account you bought with, then open Settings → Full Access → Restore Purchases. An expired or refunded purchase returns to free features; a purchase waiting for approval unlocks only once it is approved.

How do I ask for a refund?

Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com. After a refund the app returns to free features and keeps your artwork and diary.

How do invitation codes work?

Open Settings → Full Access → Redeem Invitation Code and enter an Apple offer code issued for a campaign. Redemption needs an internet connection and an Apple Account, and only an Apple-verified transaction unlocks access.

A redeemed code gives one free year of Full Access from the Apple transaction date (a calendar anniversary in UTC), then returns to free features with no renewal or charge. Each Apple Account can redeem one code per campaign; please don't share one-time codes publicly. A code does not cancel or pause an existing subscription.

Artwork, backups and new devices

Where is my artwork stored?

Every stroke saves automatically on this device. ColorBreathe has no accounts and no automatic cloud sync, and we keep no copy of your artwork or diary.

How do I back up and restore?

Open Settings → Data & Backup to export your diary, artwork or everything as a .colorbreathe file, and to import one; the import limit is 256 MB per backup. A backup can contain private diary entries and photos, so keep it somewhere safe, such as iCloud Drive in the Files app.

How do I move to a new iPhone or iPad?

Export an "everything" backup on the old device, move it with AirDrop or iCloud Drive, import it on the new device in Settings → Data & Backup, then restore purchases. Diary folders are excluded from system backups, so a device backup alone won't bring your diary across.

What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting the app deletes the artwork and diary on that device, and we can't recover anything that wasn't backed up. Export a backup first. PNG, video and text exports are finished outputs, not restorable backups.

Saving failed. What should I do?

Keep the app and your files, check free storage and try again. If it still fails, send the error text and a screenshot to 306116317@qq.com.

Coloring, importing and exporting

How do I import my own line art?

In My Work, create a blank canvas or import line art from the camera, Photos or Files. Region detection works best with closed outlines; adjust cropping, threshold or inversion, or simply paint with brushes. The camera needs a physical device and camera permission, which you can turn back on in system Settings. The photo picker only shares the images you select.

How do I export and share?

From the editor, export a PNG, a share card or a drawing replay video, or choose Save to Photos.

Diary and moods

How do I write a diary entry?

Tap Write this moment on Today; the diary icon at the top right opens all entries. A mood is optional and text alone can be saved. Each entry holds up to 500 characters and 20 attachments, including up to 9 photos. When you cancel you can keep or discard the draft, and you can edit or delete an entry later.

Images saved in an entry are independent copies, so deleting the original work or Photos item doesn't remove them from the diary.

Can I lock my diary?

Yes. Turn on the diary lock and unlock with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. The system handles verification; the app never receives biometric data.

How is Apple Health used?

It's optional and off by default. Once you turn it on and allow access, the app reads only today's latest State of Mind to suggest a mood and palette. It reads no other health data and never writes to Health. The raw data is used in memory on your device, never stored or uploaded. Coloring and mood records are for everyday relaxation, not diagnosis or treatment.

Language and accessibility

How do I switch between English and Chinese?

Settings → Language: Follow System, Simplified Chinese or English. The English name is ColorBreathe and the Chinese name is 今日漫. The Home Screen name and system permission prompts follow your iOS language.

Which accessibility features are supported?

Dynamic Type, VoiceOver and Reduce Motion. How drawing feels depends on your device and any assistive technology you use.
