ColorBreathe Terms of Use

Updated October 26, 2026, for version 2.3. Version 2.0 remains the free historical release; optional Full Access was introduced in version 2.1.

The app uses Apple's Standard End User License Agreement. Built-in drawings are prepared for this project, including AI-assisted artwork and new compositions of classic stories. Piano, marble, brush and ambient sounds are composed and synthesized by project programs. Production and source information is available in Settings → Artwork & Sound Credits. Ensure you have permission to use images you import; the app does not transfer copyright in source material.

Artwork and diaries are stored locally. Use Data & Backup to preserve important content. Read our Privacy Policy for data handling details. Support: 306116317@qq.com.

Free features and Full Access

Free access includes 12 starter drawings, one free page each day from a rotating selection of 49 pages, every brush, continued editing of works already started, diaries, backups and standard 2048-pixel PNG export without app branding. Replays and share cards include light app branding. New drawings outside the free selection, new blank canvases, photo/line-art imports and new copies of restricted works require Full Access. Full Access also includes complete assisted coloring, 4096-pixel and transparent PNGs and the option to remove app branding from replays and share cards. Required artwork-license credits remain.

A drawing becomes a continued work when you actually paint in it; merely viewing or favoriting a drawing does not unlock future access. Trial expiry never deletes your drawings or diary. Existing works stay editable, but do not unlock new copies of otherwise restricted drawings.

Plans, trial and renewal

Mainland China offers annual and lifetime plans for new purchases; other storefronts offer monthly, annual and lifetime plans. Available plans, local prices and offer eligibility are shown in the app and confirmed on Apple’s purchase confirmation sheet. Your Apple Account storefront determines the market independently of interface language. Changing storefronts does not revoke an existing lifetime entitlement.

Available subscription plans retain the 7-day free trial only when Apple confirms eligibility, once per subscription group and with your confirmation. Installing the app alone does not start a trial or a charge. After the trial, the selected subscription renews automatically at the displayed price unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends.

Lifetime access is a one-time purchase with no automatic renewal and includes future standard features and drawing collections in this app. Buying lifetime access does not cancel an existing subscription. Manage or cancel that subscription through your Apple Account to avoid overlapping charges. Family Sharing is not offered for these purchases.

Invitation: one year of Full Access

Open Settings → Full Access → Redeem Invitation Code and use an Apple offer code issued for this campaign. Redemption requires an internet connection and an Apple Account. Only an Apple-verified transaction unlocks access. Successful redemption grants one free year of Full Access from the Apple transaction date, measured as a calendar anniversary in UTC. Access returns to free features after that year, without automatic renewal or charges. Your artwork and diary remain.

The deadline for redeeming a code is separate from the one-year access period after redemption. Refer to the invitation terms and Apple's eligibility confirmation. Each Apple Account can redeem one code per offer; do not publicly share a one-time-use code. An invitation does not cancel or pause an existing auto-renewing subscription. Cancel that subscription in your Apple Account if you do not want its charges to continue.

Restore, manage and refunds

Open Settings → Full Access → Restore Purchases using the Apple Account used to buy. Manage subscriptions there or in Apple account settings. Purchase, invitation redemption and restore need access to Apple services; verified entitlements are cached on-device for offline use. An expired or refunded purchase returns to free features. If a purchase is awaiting approval, access begins only after approval. Apple manages refund requests at Report a Problem.

Artwork and playful activities

Built-in drawings include AI-assisted artwork. Sources and licenses are listed in Settings. Marble Art and the Bead Box are available outside mainland China. Their progress and keepsakes stay on the device; existing records are retained when the activities are unavailable in mainland China. These activities and mood records are for everyday relaxation and do not provide diagnosis or treatment.

Assisted coloring

Active Full Access directly provides complete assisted coloring in every storefront, including mainland China. Outside mainland China, a free user can permanently unlock all assistance for the drawing played by completing one Marble Art round on their work: clear the board or reach the time limit; leaving early does not count. The unlock is saved on this device for the drawing and its version. It does not unlock access to other drawings or grant Full Access. There is no free 10% allowance or shared-fill balance. Existing artwork and completed fills remain. Assistance applies reference colors to regions and shares the regular canvas, brushes, undo, redo, saving and export. Demo rounds and Watch It Play do not unlock assistance.
