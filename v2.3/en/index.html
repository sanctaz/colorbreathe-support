ColorBreathe: Coloring Diary

Color slowly. Keep your day.

Pick a page, add a few strokes, then keep today with a few words, a mood or a photo. ColorBreathe is a coloring diary for iPhone and iPad, and your artwork and diary stay on your device.

One page a day, one kind line

Open the app to today's drawing and a gentle line that never hurries you. Pick a mood and collect today's little lucky color; anything you started is waiting on Today, ready to continue.

14 collections, 168 drawings

A Time for Flowers, Birds on Branches, Twelve Animals through the Seasons, A Cat at Home, Women by the Garden, Woodland Companions, Cozy Corners, Sea and Sky, Seasonal Delights, Windows and Patterns, Tiny Fantasies, Country Lanes, Food and Craft and Storybook Wonder — from a single branch to a whole cottage, each with an estimated coloring time.

12 starter drawings and a daily page are free.

Not sure about colors? Follow a reference

Every drawing comes with a color reference. Drag the slider to compare line art with the colored preview, or borrow just a few of its colors and make your own version.

Full Access includes complete assisted coloring: roll the color pearl to a region and tap to fill from the reference. Outside mainland China, free users can permanently unlock assistance for that drawing by completing a Marble Art round: clear the board or reach the time limit; leaving early does not count. This unlock covers assistance for that drawing and its version on this device, and does not grant access to other drawings. Mainland China does not offer Marble Art or the Bead Box.

Worth keeping, finished or not

Write a few words, choose a mood manually, and attach today's artwork or a photo. A mood is optional, you can lock the diary, and your entries stay on your device.

Ten brushes, room to breathe on iPad

Fill, pencil, watercolor, marker, acrylic, crayon, airbrush, soft shade, mineral and eraser, with layers, palettes, undo and redo. Compatible iPads support Apple Pencil in portrait and landscape.

When you're done, export a PNG, a share card or a replay of your drawing.

Your work stays yours

No data collected

No account, no ads, no analytics SDK and no tracking.

Saved on your device

Every stroke saves automatically; export a .colorbreathe backup whenever you like and take it to a new device.

Works offline

Coloring, the diary and exports need no connection; only purchases and restores talk to Apple.

Read the Privacy Policy.

Free to download, unlock when you want

The free version includes 12 starter drawings and a daily page, every brush, the diary and backups. For the full library, blank canvases, importing your own line art, and 4096-pixel and transparent exports, choose Full Access, which also includes complete assisted coloring. Mainland China offers annual and lifetime plans for new purchases; other storefronts offer monthly, annual and lifetime plans. Eligible accounts retain the 7-day subscription trial. Plans and local prices are confirmed by Apple, independently of interface language.

Trials, renewals and cancelling are explained in Help & Support.
